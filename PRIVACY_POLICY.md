# Draft privacy policy

**Status:** Draft for legal review before production use.

**Last updated:** 2026-09-21

This draft reflects the functionality currently visible in the codebase. It
must be completed with the deployment's official contact information, hosting
locations, retention schedule, and legal review before it is used as a live
privacy notice.

## Information the service is designed to handle

The application may process an anonymous display name, a password stored as a
hash, profile preferences, support-chat messages, mood and goal entries,
resource activity, and requests submitted through professional-directory
forms. Professional application and session-request forms may include names,
email addresses, phone numbers, professional credentials, availability, and
notes.

Some preferences and session identifiers are stored in the browser using local
storage or session storage. Application data is stored through Supabase where
the deployed service is configured to use it.

## Purpose

The intended purposes are providing anonymous account access, community and
resource features, progress tools, professional-directory requests, service
safety, and administration.

## Your responsibilities

Do not post sensitive personal, medical, financial, or identifying information
in public or group chat spaces. This service should not be used to obtain
emergency help.

## Before production launch

The operator must add: the official privacy contact; data-controller details;
hosting and international-transfer details; retention and deletion periods;
user-access/correction/deletion procedures; cookie details; the legal basis
for each processing activity; and an age/guardian policy where applicable.

Do not treat this draft as a substitute for legal advice or a complete privacy
notice.
